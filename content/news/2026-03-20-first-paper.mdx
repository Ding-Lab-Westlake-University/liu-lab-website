Congratulations to **Yu Yuki DAI**, **Chenxin WEI**, **Yibo YUAN**, **Mohammed Mostafizur Rahman**, and **Ding LIU** on the publication of the lab's first paper:

**In Vivo Calcium Imaging with a Miniaturized Microscope in the Hypothalamus for Understanding Social Behaviors in Mice**

Published in the *Journal of Visualized Experiments (JoVE)*, the protocol describes miniscope calcium imaging of hypothalamic neurons in freely behaving mice during social isolation and reunion.
