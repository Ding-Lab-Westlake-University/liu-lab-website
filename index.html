The Need to Connect.

At the Systems Social Neuroscience Lab at Westlake University, led by Dr. Ding Liu, we investigate how naturalistic social behavior and social needs are represented and processed in the brain. By combining automated behavioral tracking, in vivo imaging, and electrophysiology, we aim to decode the fundamental organizational and functional principles of neural circuitry.
