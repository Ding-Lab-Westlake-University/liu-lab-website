Open Positions

We are actively recruiting motivated individuals at all levels. Please send your CV and a brief statement of research interest to liuding@westlake.edu.cn.

Postdoctoral Fellows — 2 Openings

We are actively seeking highly motivated Postdoctoral Fellows. Requirements: Ph.D. degree (or expected soon) with at least one first-author publication; expertise in patch clamp, miniscope calcium imaging, Neuropixels recording, or large-scale data analysis / modeling; passionate about science and highly collaborative.

How to Apply

Please send your CV, two reference letters (including one from your Ph.D. advisor), and your doctoral degree certificate in PDF format to liuding@westlake.edu.cn with the subject line "Postdoc Application".

Graduate Students & Visitors

We welcome applications from prospective Ph.D. students through Westlake University's official admissions process. Visiting scholars and rotation students are encouraged to reach out directly.
